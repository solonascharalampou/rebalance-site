The Studio

A practice built around your body, not the timetable.

We pair physiotherapy with Clinical Pilates in a calm studio in Engomi. Every session begins with assessment and ends with a plan — no two bodies follow the same route back to strength.

As a GHS (ΓΕΣΥ) provider, we keep sessions small and focused, so attention is never divided. You leave knowing what to do next, and why.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Draft — review with a legal advisor before publishing.

Who we are

Rebalance Physiotherapy & Clinical Pilates, Angelou Terzaki 4, Engomi, Nicosia, Cyprus. Contact: hello@rebalance.cy · 22 035001.

What we collect

When you create an account and book, we collect your name, email, booking history, and payment status (paid by Viva, card, cash, or package). We do not store your card details — payments are handled by Viva Wallet.

Why we use it

To create and manage your account, take and confirm bookings, issue receipts, contact you about your sessions (e.g. cancellations), and keep financial records as required by Cyprus law.

Legal basis (GDPR)

We process your data to perform our contract with you (managing your bookings), for our legitimate interest in running the studio, and to meet legal obligations such as tax record-keeping.

How long we keep it

Booking and payment records are kept for the period required by Cyprus tax law (currently six years). You may ask us to delete your account data that we are not legally required to retain.

Who has access

Only the studio owner and authorised staff. We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except service providers who help us operate (e.g. Viva Wallet for payments, Google Calendar for scheduling), who process it only on our instructions.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to data portability. To exercise these, contact us at hello@rebalance.cy. You may also complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.

Cookies

This site stores only what is necessary for you to sign in and book. It does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.
