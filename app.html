Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Draft — to be reviewed by a legal advisor before ID collection goes live.

1. Who we are

Rebalance Physiotherapy & Clinical Pilates is the data controller for the information described here.
Angelou Terzaki 4, Engomi, Nicosia, Cyprus · Tel 22 035001 / 96 489727 · hello@rebalance.cy

2. What we collect

Your account: name, email address, mobile number, and a password stored only as irreversible encryption — we never see your actual password. If you enable two-factor authentication, we store a key used solely to verify your login codes.

Patient record: your contact and clinical administration details may be synchronised with Cliniko so the studio can maintain one accurate patient record.

Your bookings: which sessions you booked and when, group or private, payment status and method, amount paid, receipt number, attendance (including no-shows), waitlist entries, and any promo, referral or gift voucher codes used.

Health information: notes recorded by your physiotherapist or instructor about injuries, physical conditions, treatment goals, or adaptations needed for your safety, and any GHS (ΓΕΣΥ) referral details.

We never store your card number, CVV, or bank details. Card payments are handled entirely by Stripe — your card details never reach our systems. We use no advertising cookies or third-party tracking.

3. Why we use it

To create and manage your account, verify the client record, take and confirm bookings, issue receipts, send confirmations and reminders, contact you if a class changes, and keep the financial records Cyprus law requires.

Legal basis: performing our contract with you (bookings and account), our legitimate interest in running the studio safely and securely, and our legal obligations for tax records. Health information is processed for the provision of health care under Article 9(2)(h) GDPR, with your explicit consent.

4. Health information

Notes about your injuries or condition are “special category” data under GDPR. We record only what is needed for safe, appropriate treatment. Access is limited to the studio owner and the physiotherapist or instructor directly involved in your care.

5. Who has access

Inside the studio: only the owner and authorised staff who need it to deliver your sessions.

Service providers who process data only on our instructions: Stripe (card payments), our hosting provider (stores the booking database), our email provider (sends codes, confirmations and receipts), and Google Calendar if enabled (class times and how many spaces are booked).

We never sell, rent or trade your data. We may disclose it where legally required, such as to the Cyprus tax authorities.

6. How long we keep it

Booking and payment records: six years, as Cyprus tax law requires. Treatment notes: as long as needed for your care and professional record-keeping obligations. Account details: while your account is active. Verification codes are deleted after 20 minutes; login sessions expire after 12 hours, or 30 days if you chose “keep me signed in”.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, ask us to delete it (where we are not legally required to keep it), restrict or object to how we use it, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these, contact us at hello@rebalance.cy or 22 035001 — we will respond within one month. You may also complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (dataprotection.gov.cy).

8. How we protect your data

Passwords are stored using strong one-way encryption (bcrypt). Two-factor authentication is available using Google or Microsoft Authenticator. All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS, sign-in attempts are rate-limited to prevent password guessing, portal access is restricted to authorised staff, and backups are taken regularly.

If a breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the Cyprus Commissioner within 72 hours and inform you where required.

9. Cookies

We store only what the site needs to work: a token so you stay signed in, your language preference, and a record that you accepted this policy. No advertising cookies, no tracking, no profiling.

10. Children

Our services are intended for adults. Where a minor attends, bookings are made and managed by a parent or guardian, who provides consent for any data we hold.

11. Changes

If we make significant changes we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you by email.

Note: This policy accurately describes what our booking system does, but it has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. Because we handle health information, a Cyprus legal advisor should review it before it is relied upon.
